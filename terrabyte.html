A recommendation that ignores your particular acre is only a rumour. TerraByte reads nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium from your soil health card, takes the pH, then listens to fifteen days of sky above your village — and only then names a crop.

No two readings come out alike, because no two fields are alike.
